Change contract
An authenticated current START_PLAIN USB offer can hand a desired, UP/CONNECTING
SYSTEM VPN over to that exact USB session. It preserves the user's encryption
intent and uses the existing serialized stop/start lifecycle. Off, DOWN, ERROR,
DISCONNECTING, an already USB-active controller, pending unapplied configuration,
and missing/wrong sessions do not initiate this handover. Losing/replacing the
session aborts its use. No Android permission or profile/preference write is added.
